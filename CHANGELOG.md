# Changelog

## v0.6.0 — 23 November 2026
- **Module 6: Tool wear evolution and cutting force in CNC end milling.** Flank wear VB in the edge coefficients; forces, power and specific energy versus VB; illustrative wear-growth model and tool-replacement plan (ISO 8688-2 VB 0.3 mm criterion, adjustable); cutting-edge close-up; Stage A/B/C content and production problem; force-vs-VB and wear-rate fitting.
- 146 verification tests.

## v0.5.0 — 23 November 2026
- **Module 5: Workpiece movement and fixture stiffness in milling.** Rigid block on a 3-2-1 fixture with compliant, compression-only contacts and Coulomb friction; lift-off and slip detection; clamping safety factor; three fixture layouts, clamp forces, end clamp, pad friction and feed direction; slot-position and depth errors in the datum frame; fixture natural frequencies; clamping-force study; Stage A/B/C content, production problem, push-test and CMM validation, worksheet.
- 129 verification tests.

## v0.4.0 — 22 November 2026
- **Module 4: Cutting-force components in CNC turning of slender shafts.** Fc/Ff/Fp with a nose-radius equivalent edge, beam FE shaft with chuck / tailstock / centres / steady rest, diameter-error profile, power, torque and theoretical roughness, lathe scene in the ISO lathe frame, Stage A/B/C content, production problem, dynamometer and micrometer validation, worksheet.
- 109 verification tests.

## v0.3.0 — 22 November 2026
- **Module 3: Chatter and dynamic instability.** Separate dynamic model: tool modes, FRF and dynamic stiffness, ZOA stability lobes cross-checked by a time-domain regenerative simulation (tooth jump-out, multiple regeneration), forced vibration vs chatter by once-per-tooth sampling and spectrum, chatter-mark visualisation, tap-test fitting and measured cuts on the lobe diagram, Stage A/B/C content, production problem and worksheet.
- 88 verification tests.

## v0.2.0 — 22 November 2026
- **Module 2: Tool bending in deep-pocket machining.** Stepped-cantilever tool (shank, reduced neck, flutes) with holder/spindle compliance; rigid deep pocket; full-depth, step-down, spring-pass and deflection-compensation strategies; tool deflection, bending moment and stress charts; wall deviation profile; Stage A/B/C content and production problem; worksheet.
- Module selector in the header (`#module-2` link).
- Student-first README with live app link, screenshots and a Malay quick guide.
- GitHub Pages deployment workflow.
- 68 verification tests.

## v0.1.0 — 22 November 2026
- **Module 1: Thin-wall milling.** Mechanistic milling-force model (straight/helical flutes), plate finite-element wall with stiffness updated from the stock, force–deflection coupling, four pass strategies, 3D lab, synchronised graphs, Stage A/B/C learning and assessment, validation upload, CSV/report export, worksheet.
- 47 verification tests.
